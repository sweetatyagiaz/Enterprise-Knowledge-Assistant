# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in Enterprise Knowledge Assistant respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Welcome questions from beginners.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish someone else's confidential information.
- Follow the project's security-reporting guidance for vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Reports should be made privately to the repository maintainers.

This code of conduct applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
